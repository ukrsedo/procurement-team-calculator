What if I change staffing?

Edit total FTEs across the organisation, one at a time. SLA recalculates automatically. 1 FTE is one full-time position; 0.5 FTE is half-time. Procurement time is applied separately.

Staffing rule: senior employees cannot exceed 50% of total planned headcount. Managers are a separate role and count in the total. Part-time positions count as people for this rule.

FTEs are allocated across teams as evenly as possible, using whole positions before a part-time position. With separate queues a one-FTE change affects one team; shared workload makes that capacity available across teams. Management effectiveness is outside this procurement SLA model.

Personal productivity & procurement time

Maximum personal productivity is the maximum number of parallel cases at full availability. Procurement time sets daily work hours after management and routine. A new hire's productivity factor reduces work hours during ramp-up; it is not deducted a second time from the parallel-case limit.

Absences, turnover & new hires

Leave is split into two periods. Vacation and sickness episodes do not overlap. Handover uses 50% of both the sender's and receiver's normal procurement hours; simultaneous handovers add up. Work is reassigned at the start of leave or on departure, under the selected cross-team support policy. During sickness, owned work waits for the employee's return. Managers can also leave and are replaced in the same role.

Additional sickness applies to a randomly chosen share of the planned workforce each year, using stochastic rounding. Turnover is an annual departure probability per position. A vacancy has zero capacity; after hiring, the replacement works at the selected productivity for the ramp-up period. Calendar months are added as actual months on a synthetic calendar; weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Holiday dates are illustrative.

Effort, review & external waits
ProcedureSenior effort, hJunior effort, hJunior review, hExternal wait min / max, calendar days

Managers use the same effort assumptions as seniors. Effort varies by ±20% per request; 55% takes place before the external wait, 45% after. Waits are uniform within the range. Waiting cases occupy a parallel-case slot but consume no work hours. Any work performed by a junior triggers the specified review effort from a senior or manager. Seniors and managers prioritise their own work and reviews by the nearest deadline. Juniors are preferred for Single-source, RFQ and extensions; seniors for RFPs.

Search limits & simulation

Each scenario has a warm-up year, a year of arrivals to measure and a further year of observation. Incoming work continues throughout; there is no quiet period to clear the queue. The same demand streams are used across team compositions. All cases received in the measurement year are included; those still incomplete fail SLA. Types with zero demand are excluded from the target check. P90 treats incomplete cases as taking longer than observed completions; it is withheld when the percentile cannot be estimated.

Cost optimisation finds the lowest-cost staffing that meets the target for every active procedure. SLA optimisation maximises the lowest compliance across active procedures within the salary budget. Cost breaks SLA ties. Search uses whole organisation-wide FTEs; manual scenarios support quarter-FTE steps. It is not proof of a global optimum or a guarantee for every future year. Part-time positions have proportionally fewer hours and parallel-case slots, rounded down to whole cases. Sickness and turnover probabilities apply per person, including part-time staff. The model uses eight-hour working days, no overtime and the selected cross-team support policy. Salary is budgeted for filled and vacant positions throughout the year. Replacement workers inherit the role's planned absence calendar. Procurement hours do not cover managerial performance or recruitment work.

Reported compliance pools all measured requests. It is distinct from the probability that every active procedure meets its target in an individual simulated year. Annual ranges below represent simulated variation under fixed inputs; they do not include uncertainty about the inputs themselves.
